## Brand & Style

This design system channels the precision, drive, and high-stakes intensity of competitive FIRST LEGO League (FLL) engineering. Rooted in the visual legacy of the team mascot—a mechanical cobalt guardian bearing competition-grade red insignia—the aesthetic merges technical mission-control telemetry with high-performance digital cockpits.

The visual style blends **Corporate / Modern High-Tech** with subtle **Telemetry Brutalism**:
- Deep petroleum navy foundations provide an immersive, focused operating canvas.
- Crisp white technical overlays supply high-legibility telemetry and structural markers.
- High-voltage competition crimson serves as a decisive action and urgency trigger, mirroring pit-lane dashboards and real-time match countdowns.
- The interface feels rigorous, calculated, and robust—engineered for students, mentors, and judges analyzing telemetry, sensor calibration, and run scores under pressure.

## Colors

The dark-mode palette is anchored in industrial blue and intense competition red, balanced by high-contrast whites and multi-tiered obsidian-navy hulls.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#005a9c`)**: The structural chassis tone. Used for interactive states, key system containers, active tab indicators, and telemetry accents.
- **Secondary (`#e51a24`)**: The competition drive accent. Reserved strictly for high-impact callouts, live match timers, mission-critical toggles, record scores, and alert statuses.
- **Tertiary / Base Light (`#ffffff`)**: Pure optical white for high-priority numbers, vital vector readouts, and core primary typography against dark substrates.
- **Neutral Core (`#09101a`)**: The deep vacuum base. Establishes an ultra-dark navy surface that eliminates glare in competition pits.

### Surface System
- **Canvas Base (`#09101a`)**: Background canvas for all primary screens and consoles.
- **Chassis Level 1 (`#0f1a26`)**: Elevated panels, sidebar navigation, and module cards.
- **Chassis Level 2 (`#162233`)**: Active card states, modal dialogues, input basins, and nested inspector units.
- **Structural Border (`#22354c`)**: Muted structural gridlines, divider rules, and low-contrast perimeter strokes.

## Typography

Typography establishes an engineering cockpit atmosphere by coupling the angular, tech-forward cadence of **Space Grotesk** with the utilitarian precision of **Inter**.

### Roles & Execution
- **Display & Headlines (`Space Grotesk`)**: Render mission titles, telemetry readouts, scores, and primary metrics. Monospace-like numerical proportions provide immediate tabular clarity.
- **Body & Continuous Copy (`Inter`)**: Deployed across code snippets, field logs, rule descriptions, and mission checklists to guarantee zero reading fatigue in ambient conditions.
- **Telemetry Labels (`Space Grotesk`, All Caps)**: Sub-labels, metadata pins, and status flags use uppercase micro-typography with wide tracking (`0.04em` to `0.08em`) to mimic aerospace control hardware.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is constructed on an 8px base rhythm that adapts to dashboard-dense grids and multi-sensor inspection feeds.

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12-column fluid grid with `2rem` outer margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Optimized for dual-panel mission control: tactical scoring on the left, telemetry and code maps on the right.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8-column layout with `1.5rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Panels collapse into vertical stacked modules with persistent sticky control heads.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column layout with `1rem` margins and `0.75rem` gutters. Sensor blocks and action triggers stretch full-width for single-handed field operation.

### Density Rules
Padding within components scales from compact (`space-xs` and `space-sm`) for dense numerical counters and pin chips, up to expansive (`space-xl`) between distinct operational units on the arena board overview.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy prioritizes crisp tactile modularity rather than exaggerated drop shadows. The system uses a disciplined **Tonal Layer + Technical Keyline** approach.

### Tier Structure
- **Level 0 (Floor)**: `#09101a` — The overall frame backdrop. No shadow.
- **Level 1 (Panels & Cards)**: `#0f1a26` enclosed by a continuous 1px hairline stroke in `#22354c`. This creates an encapsulated "hardware module" boundary.
- **Level 2 (Popovers & Modals)**: `#162233` with a subtle technical drop glow: `0 8px 24px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.65)`, complemented by a 1px primary stroke (`#005a9c`) at 40% opacity.
- **Level 3 (Tactical Overlays & Scrims)**: Semi-translucent `#09101a` at 85% opacity with a `12px` backdrop blur, keeping background field data visible yet non-distracting.

### Accent Emittance
Key indicators and primary buttons utilize an optical sub-glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 16px rgba(0, 90, 156, 0.4)` or secondary emergency `0 0 16px rgba(229, 26, 36, 0.45)`) to simulate illuminated hardware switches on competitive robotics arenas.

## Shapes

The design system adopts **Roundedness Level 2 (`0.5rem` / `8px`)** to reflect modern industrial robotics casing—robust, functional, and devoid of overly playful soft radii.

### Scale Mapping
- **Micro Radii (`rounded-sm` / `0.25rem`)**: Inner indicators, data badges, and terminal tags.
- **Standard Radii (`rounded` / `0.5rem`)**: Primary buttons, input controls, metric widgets, and list items.
- **Container Radii (`rounded-lg` / `1rem`)**: Mission boards, telemetry modules, and elevated modal chassis.
- **Boundary Caps (`rounded-xl` / `1.5rem`)**: Structural dashboard shells and parent display cards.

The mascot's clean, robotic geometry is echoed through geometric consistency: structural corners stay crisp and predictable, avoiding full pill shapes except for circular status beacons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Drive Blue)**: Solid `#005a9c` background with pure white typography (`#ffffff`, Space Grotesk Bold). Border radius `0.5rem`. Hover state introduces a luminance rise to `#0073c7` and an active box-shadow glow (`0 0 12px rgba(0, 90, 156, 0.5)`).
- **Secondary Alert (Competition Red)**: Solid `#e51a24` background with pure white text for match start/stop, score finalization, and penalty warnings. Hover transitions to `#ff2a35`.
- **Ghost / Outline**: Transparent fill with 1.5px border in `#22354c`, shifting to `#005a9c` on hover with text in `#ffffff`.

### Chips & Status Indicators
- **Autonomous Ready Chip**: Background `#0f1a26`, border `1px solid #22354c`, Space Grotesk `label-sm` text. Prefixed by a pulsing 6px circular dot (`#005a9c` or `#e51a24` depending on match status).
- **Match Penalty Tag**: Solid crimson `#e51a24` fill, white bold mono-labeling, zero margin collapse.

### Input Fields
- **Data Basin**: `#0f1a26` background, `1px solid #22354c` border, `0.5rem` radius. Text rendered in `#ffffff` via Inter `body-md`. 
- **Focus State**: Border shifts abruptly to `#005a9c` accompanied by a 2px outer keyline glow in `rgba(0, 90, 156, 0.25)`.
- **Error / Fault State**: Border switches to `#e51a24` with contextual error micro-copy underneath in secondary red.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Box size `20px x 20px`, `rounded-sm` (`4px`), border `1.5px solid #22354c`, background `#09101a`.
- Checked state fills with `#005a9c` and presents an angular pure-white checkmark icon. Radio selections utilize an inner concentric circle of `#e51a24` when triggering competitive penalties.

### Cards & Chassis Panels
- **Telemetry Card**: Background `#0f1a26`, border `1px solid #22354c`, internal padding `space-lg`. Top edge features an optional 2px primary blue accent stripe to signal active motor or gyro telemetry streams.
- **Match Scoreboard Card**: Employs deep `#162233` surface elevation with an explicit Space Grotesk display number in pure `#ffffff`, flanked by crimson `#e51a24` mission milestone pills.

### Custom Component: Run Timer & Mission Matrix
- **Dual-State Stopwatch**: Fixed-width tabular digits in Space Grotesk `display-lg`, flashing to secondary red `#e51a24` during the final 30 seconds of an FLL table run.
- **Sensor Matrix Toggle**: Segmented toggle buttons housed within a unified `#09101a` track, providing haptic visual switching between Optical, Gyro, and Ultrasonic sensor logs.
